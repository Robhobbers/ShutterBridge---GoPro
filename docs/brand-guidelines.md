# IbexFuel Brand Guidelines v0.1

> Last updated: 2026-10-01  
> Status: Selected identity direction

## Brand in one line

**Fuel Your Climb.**

IbexFuel makes endurance nutrition easier to understand and more affordable by turning current sports-science evidence into transparent products and practical guidance, without inflated claims or unnecessary complexity.

## Purpose

### Mission

Make evidence-led sports nutrition understandable and accessible to everyday and competitive endurance athletes.

### Vision

A world where athletes can fuel long efforts confidently without needing a sports-science degree or paying for marketing hype.

### Brand promise

Every product and recommendation should answer four questions plainly:

1. What is in it?
2. How much should an athlete use?
3. What does the evidence support?
4. What does it cost per hour of training or racing?

### Positioning

IbexFuel is an evidence-led endurance nutrition brand for runners, trail runners, cyclists and triathletes who want simple, dependable fuelling. It replaces crowded labels and vague performance claims with transparent formulas, practical guidance and fair pricing.

## Audience

### Primary audience

Everyday endurance athletes training or racing for roughly one to 24 hours. They care about performance but do not want nutrition to become another complicated hobby.

### Secondary audience

Experienced and competitive athletes who already understand carbohydrate, sodium and hydration targets, and value transparent formulations that let them make their own decisions.

### Shared needs

- Clear guidance by duration, intensity and conditions
- Ingredients and quantities shown without proprietary-blend language
- Practical explanations of the science
- Affordable cost per serving and cost per hour
- Products that work together across pre-, during- and post-exercise use

## Messaging

### Primary message

**Fuel Your Climb.**

Every athlete has a climb: a first finish line, a personal best, a long day in the mountains or the discipline to keep training. IbexFuel helps them fuel the effort with clear, evidence-led nutrition.

### Supporting messages

- **Know what you are taking.** Every ingredient and quantity is explained.
- **Fuel the effort in front of you.** Guidance scales from a one-hour session to an all-day race.
- **Pay for the formula, not the hype.** Useful nutrition should be accessible.
- **Evidence changes. We keep up.** Guidance should reflect the weight of current research and change when the evidence does.
- **Simple fuel. Proven science.** The emotional promise is supported by a clear reason to believe.

### Short pitch

IbexFuel turns sports-nutrition science into simple products and clear fuelling guidance for endurance athletes.

### Longer pitch

Most endurance products are built from similar established ingredients, then surrounded by complicated labels and expensive marketing. IbexFuel starts with the evidence: transparent formulas, useful doses, clear explanations and fair pricing for training and racing from one hour to 24 hours.

### Tagline territory

Brand slogan: **Fuel Your Climb.**

Proof line: **Simple fuel. Proven science.**

Supporting campaign lines:

- Fuel without the fluff.
- Know what powers your effort.
- The formula should not be a secret.
- Built for the long effort.

Avoid absolute or hard-to-prove claims such as “best”, “ultimate”, “scientifically perfect”, “guaranteed performance” and “revolutionary”.

## Voice

| Trait | We sound like | We do not sound like |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence-led | Clear about what research supports and how strong it is | A white coat used as decoration |
| Direct | Plain language, useful numbers and specific recommendations | Dense jargon or vague inspiration |
| Honest | Open about trade-offs, uncertainty and individual tolerance | Certain about evidence that is still developing |
| Encouraging | A knowledgeable training partner | A lecturer or elite-only coach |
| Adventurous | Comfortable in the real world of long days and changing conditions | Extreme for the sake of looking extreme |

### Writing rules

- Lead with the practical answer, then explain the science.
- Use grams, milligrams, millilitres and cost per hour where relevant.
- Separate established evidence, reasonable practice and emerging evidence.
- Explain technical terms on first use.
- Prefer “may help” or “evidence supports” when certainty is limited.
- Never hide useful information behind proprietary language.

## Visual direction: summit quest

The visual identity combines a retro adventure-game spirit with the clarity of a modern nutrition label. The ibex on the summit represents the athlete meeting their own challenge. Pixel and game references make the brand welcoming and memorable; clean typography, open formulas and disciplined information design carry the scientific credibility.

The retro layer should appear in the mascot, progress markers, route selectors and occasional illustrations. Dense science, dosage and safety information stays clean and highly readable. Avoid turning every heading and control into pixel art.

### Colour palette

| Name | Hex | RGB | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alpine ink | `#16352F` | 22, 53, 47 | Primary brand colour, headings, dark surfaces |
| Mineral white | `#F5F7F1` | 245, 247, 241 | Main background |
| Glacier | `#CFE9E3` | 207, 233, 227 | Information panels and science content |
| Signal orange | `#F06432` | 240, 100, 50 | Calls to action and energetic highlights |
| Lichen | `#A6BE75` | 166, 190, 117 | Secondary natural accent |
| Graphite | `#202925` | 32, 41, 37 | Body text |

Signal orange should be used sparingly. Alpine ink and mineral white carry the brand; glacier and lichen organise educational content.

### Typography

- **Display:** Barlow Condensed, 600–700. Compact, athletic and useful for strong headlines and pack labels.
- **Body:** Atkinson Hyperlegible, 400–700. Clear at small sizes and already present in the website.
- **Data:** IBM Plex Mono, 400–600. Use for quantities, ingredient tables, evidence labels and cost-per-hour figures.

Headlines should be short and left aligned. Data should look readable and intentional, not like decorative computer code.

### Signature devices

- **Summit sprite:** a friendly ibex standing on a peak, drawn on a restrained pixel grid.
- **The climb line:** a line that can act as an elevation profile, progress path or evidence graph.
- **Effort levels:** duration ranges can behave like game levels—clear stages of a longer journey rather than competitive rankings.

Gamification should reward learning, planning and consistency. It should not imply that fuelling has one perfect score or encourage unsafe behaviour.

### Photography

- Real athletes in real conditions, especially trail, road, gravel and transition areas
- Natural light, visible weather and honest effort
- Close details of bottles, food, hands, sweat and preparation
- Space in the composition for useful labels or dosage information
- A mix of everyday and competitive athletes

Avoid staged victory poses, neon powder explosions, laboratory stock photos and heavily processed “extreme” imagery.

## Logo system

### Selected direction — Summit sprite

A compact pixel-art ibex stands on a mountain peak beside a clean, bold `IBEXFUEL` wordmark. This balances playfulness with credibility and stays flexible across the website, packaging and app icons.

The orange horns and base line carry the energetic accent. The mountain and body use the quieter alpine palette so the mark remains readable beside scientific and nutritional information.

### Supporting badge direction

A fuller “Quest badge” may be used for stickers, events and community challenges. It is a campaign graphic rather than the primary company logo.

### Approved variants

| Variant | Use |
|---|---|
| Primary horizontal | Website headers, packaging fronts and documents |
| Stacked | Square placements, posters and social graphics |
| Summit sprite mark | Avatars, favicon, app icon and small spaces |
| Reversed | Alpine-ink or other approved dark backgrounds |
| Monochrome | One-colour printing, embossing and manufacturing marks |

### Logo usage

- Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least one quarter of the summit sprite's width.
- Use the full horizontal logo at 180 px wide or larger.
- Use the standalone sprite below 180 px; do not include the proof line at small sizes.
- Use only approved palette colours.
- Do not smooth the sprite pixels, add gradients, rotate the mark or place it over a busy photograph.
- “Fuel Your Climb.” may be omitted when space is limited. “Simple fuel. Proven science.” is supporting copy and is not required inside every logo lockup.

### Suggested information architecture

- **Fuel guide:** practical guidance by duration and sport
- **Science:** readable evidence summaries with references and review dates
- **Products:** pre-, during- and post-exercise products as they are developed
- **About:** mission, principles and transparent development process
- **Projects:** a low-emphasis home for unrelated technical side projects

## ShutterBridge separation

ShutterBridge is a technical side project, not an IbexFuel nutrition product. It may borrow IbexFuel's base typography and layout system temporarily, but it should have its own name, mark and accent colour.

Recommended hosting structure:

- Main nutrition brand: `ibexfuel.com`
- Technical projects: `projects.ibexfuel.com` or `labs.ibexfuel.com`
- Individual project: `projects.ibexfuel.com/<project-name>`

Do not place the project in the primary IbexFuel product navigation. Label the relationship plainly: “An independent project hosted by IbexFuel.”

Before renaming ShutterBridge, check candidate names for existing software, products, domains and trademarks. The new name should describe remote camera control or flight-camera triggering rather than reference nutrition.
